About Us

Three generations through the same door

The practice sits on Lawes Street in East Maitland. Dr Nish, with nearly ten years behind him, has a particular interest in veneers, wisdom teeth, dentures and full-mouth rehabilitation. Dr Nav is a Fellow of the Royal Australasian College of Dental Surgeons. Zoe covers preventative and general care for every age, and Penny has been at the front desk since 2019.

We live and work in this part of the Hunter, sponsor local sport, and count several families among our patients who are now on their third generation with us.
